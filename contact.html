Contact

Get in touch

Bug reports, wrong results, missing tests, broken links — all welcome. This form goes straight to my inbox, and because the reply address is set to yours, hitting “Reply” on my answer comes back to you.

Send a message

Fill in the subject and message and press send. Your name is optional, but an email address is needed so I can reply to you.

The form posts to Web3Forms, a third-party service that forwards the message to my inbox. Your details are used to answer you and are not stored on this site. The privacy policy has the details.

What is most useful to report

Diagnostics are easy to get subtly wrong, and a bad result is worse than no result. These are the details that let a problem be reproduced and fixed:

  • Which test, and what it displayed
  • What you expected it to display instead
  • Your browser and operating system
  • The device, and how it is connected
  • Whether the problem is consistent or intermittent

The latency reading, if the fault is a delay

InputScope is a small project run in spare time, so replies usually take a few days. Genuinely broken measurements and wrong explanations get priority, because a wrong number causes people to chase a hardware fault that does not exist.

Something urgent?

InputScope cannot repair hardware, recover data, or give support for a specific product. If your keyboard is trapping liquid, stop using it and get it serviced. If a machine is behaving dangerously, a diagnostic page is not the right tool.
