Privacy Policy

The short version: the diagnostic tools measure things in your browser and send nothing anywhere. There is no account, no analytics script on the tool pages, and no database behind this site.

1. Who runs this site

InputScope is an independent project. Questions about this policy can be sent to hello@inputscope.com or through the contact form.

2. The diagnostic tools

The mouse and keyboard tests work by listening to events inside your browser tab — pointer movements, key presses, wheel scrolls — and calculating the results on your own device. Specifically:

  • No measurement result is transmitted to a server. There is no upload, no submission, no telemetry, and no API call that carries a result.
  • Results are held in the memory of the page and are discarded when the tab is closed or reloaded.
  • No test result, keystroke or pointer coordinate is ever read by the site operator.
  • The tools do not request access to the microphone, camera, clipboard, files or location, and they do not use any advertising or tracking technology on the measurement pages.

The only exception worth naming is the browser's own copy-and-paste button on each tool, which puts the result text on your clipboard so you can paste it somewhere. That is done by your browser, under your control, and the clipboard content is not sent to this site.

3. Review mode

This site includes an optional review mode used while the site is being built and improved. When it is switched on, you can click any element and attach a note. Those notes are stored in your browser's localStorage on your own device so they survive a page reload, and they are never transmitted anywhere. The review panel provides a button that copies the notes as text so you can paste them wherever you like — again, that is a local clipboard action.

Review mode is not active for ordinary visitors. It is switched on by adding a specific parameter to the page address, or by a button that appears once you have used it deliberately.

4. Server logs

Like any website, the server that hosts these files may keep standard access logs for operational and security purposes. These typically record the requested address, the time of the request, the browser's user agent string and the referring page. They are used to keep the site running and to detect abuse. They are not combined with any measurement data, because no measurement data ever leaves your browser.

5. Cookies and advertising

At the time of writing, InputScope uses no cookies of its own and runs no advertising or analytics scripts. Should advertising be introduced in future, it would be limited to the informational pages, and this section would be updated before it goes live along with a cookie notice and a consent mechanism where one is required.

If advertising is served, third-party vendors, including Google, may use cookies to serve ads based on your prior visits to this and other websites. You can opt out of personalised advertising through Google Ads Settings, or opt out of third-party vendor cookie use for personalised advertising at aboutads.info.

6. Third-party resources

This site loads two typefaces from Google Fonts, which means your browser makes a request to Google's servers and Google receives the usual technical information, including your IP address. If you would rather avoid that, the pages are fully readable with the fonts blocked; a content-blocking extension will not break anything here.

Links to other websites are provided for convenience. Once you follow one, that site's own privacy policy applies, and InputScope has no control over it.

7. Children's privacy

This site is a general-audience technical tool and is not directed at children. Because no personal information is collected from anyone, there is nothing collected from children either.

8. Your rights

Because no personal data is collected, there is generally nothing for this site to hand over, correct or delete. If you contact this site by email, that message and the reply are kept only as long as it takes to deal with your enquiry and to keep a record of the fix, if one was needed. Requests can be sent to hello@inputscope.com.

9. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published on this page with a revised date. Material changes — particularly anything involving data collection or advertising — will be called out on the site's main page.

10. Contact

Questions about privacy, data or advertising on this site can be sent to hello@inputscope.com or through the contact form.
