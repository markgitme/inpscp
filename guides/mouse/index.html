Mouse troubleshooting

Six faults account for almost every mouse complaint. Start with the symptom that matches yours, and each guide will tell you which measurement separates the likely causes.

Written by Mark · Updated 28 October 2026

Start with the symptom

Six faults account for almost every mouse complaint

Each guide below opens with the measurement that separates the likely causes, then gives the fix in the order you should try it.

Why is my mouse not double-clicking?

Usually a double-click window that is too tight, occasionally a worn switch. The guide has the fix for each, and a test that measures your actual interval.

Why does my mouse click on its own?

A switch that is making contact at rest, or a wireless link reporting a stale button state. The click tester tells you which.

Why is my mouse unresponsive or not moving?

Asleep, on the wrong input, driver problem, or dead. Four faults that all look identical until you test them.

Why is my scroll wheel skipping or jittering?

A worn scroll encoder, usually the first part of a mouse to fail. How to clean it, and how to tell it apart from a bad surface.

Why does my cursor jump or freeze randomly?

A dirty sensor throwing the pointer, or a wireless link renegotiating. The pattern of the jump tells you which one it is.

Why are my mouse side buttons not working?

Almost never broken hardware. The buttons fire fine; the operating system just has no instruction for what to do with them.

Triage

Symptom, likely cause, where to look

The short version, for when you already know what is wrong and just want the setting that fixes it.

SymptomLikely causeWhere to look
Cursor skips or stops at screen edgesWindows pointer acceleration or a driver-level speed settingMouse settings → pointer speed; also the “enhance pointer precision” toggle
Double-click selects nothingDouble-click speed window too tight, or a slow switchMouse settings → double-click speed slider
Scroll skips backwardsWorn scroll encoderPhysical cleaning; usually needs replacement
Cursor stutters on a small section of the screenDirt on the sensor, or a surface defectClean the lens; try a different pad
Right-click opens the wrong menuSecondary-click mapping overrideMouse settings → “open the context menu on” → right button
Middle-click pastes instead of opening a new tabMiddle-click remapped in the browserBrowser extension settings, or a custom mouse macro utility
Movement feels laggy but the mouse is fineWireless interference or a low batteryMove the receiver, charge the mouse, or use the cable
Side buttons do nothingDriver not installed, or buttons mapped to something elseDevice manager; the manufacturer's configuration software
