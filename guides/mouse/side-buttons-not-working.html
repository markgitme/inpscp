Why are my mouse side buttons not working?

Side buttons are almost never handled by the operating system. They fire; they just have no instruction for what to do.

Written by Mark · Updated 28 October 2026

The test that settles it

Press each thumb button and watch the violet tabs on the diagram. If they light, the hardware is fine and you need mapping software. Run the click tester

Side buttons are almost never handled by the operating system directly. They are reported as extra mouse buttons, and mapping them to something useful — browser back and forward being the common default — needs either the manufacturer's configuration software or a generic remapping utility. On a machine with no such software, the buttons still physically fire but the browser has no instruction for what to do with them.

That distinction is exactly what the click tester makes visible. If the violet thumb tabs light up when you press them, the hardware is fine and you need software to bind them. If they stay dark, the problem is the button or the wireless pairing, not the mapping.

How to read it

What the click tester means

The violet tabs on the left flank of the diagram light when the side buttons fire. That single observation splits the fault in two.

One observation — do the tabs light? — separates hardware from mapping.

ReadingThresholdWhat it means
The violet tabs light upevents receivedThe hardware works. The buttons simply have no instruction — install mapping software
The violet tabs stay dark0 eventsThe buttons are not firing. A driver, a pairing problem, or a faulty switch
They light, but do nothing usefulevents received, no actionThe browser has them bound to nothing. Remap them in the browser or in software
What to try

In order

Check the diagram

Press each thumb button in turn and watch the violet tabs. This tells you whether you have a mapping problem or a hardware one.

Check the browser

Middle and side buttons are remappable in the browser. Look for a mouse-button extension, and check that nothing has bound them to something else.

Install the maker's software

Logitech, Razer, SteelSeries and the rest all ship configuration software that binds the extra buttons. Without it, the buttons fire and nothing happens.

Check the driver

Device Manager → Mice and other pointing devices. A missing or stale driver stops the extra buttons being reported at all.
