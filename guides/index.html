Mouse settings that change your test results

The Windows and macOS switches that move your numbers — and how to use one as a controlled experiment.

Why is my mouse not double-clicking?

The double-click window is usually too tight rather than the mouse being broken. Measure your real interval, then raise the setting.

Why does my mouse click on its own?

Three things cause it: a worn switch, a wireless link reporting a stale button state, or software injecting clicks. The click tester tells you which.

Why is my mouse unresponsive or not moving?

Four faults look identical until you test them: asleep, wrong input, driver, or dead. The click tester tells you which.

Why is my scroll wheel skipping or jittering?

A wheel that skips or moves in uneven steps is almost always a worn scroll encoder — the first part of most mice to fail.

Why does my cursor jump or freeze randomly?

The pointer teleporting across the screen has two classic causes: a dirty sensor throwing it, or a wireless link renegotiating.

Why are my mouse side buttons not working?

Side buttons are almost never handled by the operating system. They fire; they just have no instruction for what to do.
