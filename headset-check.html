Headset Check

The headphone half of a headset. Play the left channel, then the right, and mark what you heard — the drawing lights with whichever side is playing, and goes dark when the tone stops.

The ear cup lights while its channel is playing, and goes dark when the tone stops. Mark a side Silent and it turns red and stays that way, because a side that never played is a different problem from one that played in the wrong ear.

The tone check chooses what the channel check plays. Pick a tone and a sound, then use Left, Right or Both above to hear it — choosing on its own makes no sound. The string note is generated in the browser rather than recorded, so it is the same note every time and nothing is downloaded.

A tone that is missing at one end is usually the headphones rolling off there rather than a fault — and it is usually your hearing as well. Start low and work up.
